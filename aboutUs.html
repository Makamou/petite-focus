About Me

I am Moubarak Akamou — a photographer and front-end developer devoted to capturing meaningful moments and crafting refined digital experiences.

My photography work spans portraits, landscapes and candid lifestyle imagery, with a steady focus on authentic storytelling and timeless composition. I seek to preserve the essence of connection and emotion in every frame — quiet, deliberate, true.

In parallel, I bring more than two years of experience as a front-end developer, building responsive and accessible web applications with JavaScript, React, HTML, CSS and Tailwind. Currently pursuing a Master’s in Cloud Computing Systems, I am committed to scalable, high-performance solutions that pair technical precision with thoughtful design.

Photography and code are two mediums I use to convey clarity, narrative, and lasting impact. Thank you for visiting — I welcome opportunities to collaborate and connect.

— Moubarak
